# Contributing Public Records

Contributions are welcome. This is a moderated archive, not an unverified document dump.

## What to submit

Suitable submissions include official records obtained through:

- Louisiana Public Records Law requests
- Federal FOIA requests
- Official government websites or portals
- Court dockets
- Public meeting packets, agendas, and minutes
- Other identifiable public-body sources

## Required information

With each submission, provide as much of the following as is known:

- **Agency / public body**
- **Record type** (PRR response, FOIA response, agenda, minutes, court filing, permit, report, etc.)
- **Subject**
- **Request date**, if applicable
- **Response date**, if applicable
- **Date range covered**, if known
- **Source / provenance**
- **Requester**, if applicable
- **Whether the file is exactly as received**
- **Any known agency redactions or withholding explanation**

Do not editorialize the document title or filename.

## Submission standard

A moderator must be able to reasonably establish that the document is what the contributor represents it to be. Screenshots, excerpts, copied text, or unattributed files may be rejected when the original record or provenance cannot be established.

Whenever possible, submit the original PDF or electronic file supplied by the public body.

## Privacy and restricted information

Before submitting, check the record for information that may be legally restricted or inappropriate to republish, including protected archaeological-site locations, sensitive personal identifiers, protected health information, credentials, or other nonpublic data.

Do not alter an original merely to make it look cleaner. If redaction is necessary for publication, identify the file as a publication/redacted copy and explain the redaction in the collection metadata.

## Records are not accusations

This repository preserves source material. A document may contain allegations, claims, correspondence, preliminary findings, litigation positions, or statements that have not been adjudicated or independently verified. Collection descriptions should remain factual and neutral.

## Corrections and supplemental productions

If an agency later provides corrected, replacement, or supplemental records, submit them as additional material and identify their relationship to the earlier production. Do not silently overwrite materially different records.

## How to contribute

Open a GitHub issue describing the material before submitting a pull request, or contact 337.NEWS through its published contact channels if you do not use GitHub.

By contributing, you represent that you are providing material you reasonably believe may lawfully be made public.
