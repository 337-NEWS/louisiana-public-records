# Local topics: start with the documents

Looking for the records behind a local conversation? Pick the name people are using. Each link opens a collection or topic page with downloadable source files. This is a reading guide, not a ranking of what is most popular or a claim that every related record has been collected.

**Updated October 13, 2026.** Agendas describe items scheduled for consideration; filings and requests do not establish that a project was approved or that an allegation is true.

## Orion / Persimmon solar project

[Open the Persimmon Energy / Persimmon Gulch page](indexes/projects/persimmon-energy.md). It links to Orion Renewables' project-page print and visual simulations. These are company materials; this archive does not establish a permit decision or construction status.

## SpaceX / Starbase Louisiana / Pecan Island

[Open the Starbase Louisiana page](indexes/projects/starbase-louisiana.md). It links to FAA and Army Corps FOIA requests for records concerning the Pecan Island area. The requested agencies' responses are not archived here yet.

## City of Sulphur: council meetings and city finances

[Browse Sulphur City Council agendas, packets, minutes, resolutions, and an ordinance](municipal/sulphur/city-council/2026/README.md), or [open the city's FY2025 financial audit](municipal/sulphur/annual-financial-report-fy2025/README.md). Check minutes or other later records before treating an agenda item as an adopted action.

## BECI / Beauregard Electric

[Open the Beauregard Electric guide](indexes/organizations/beauregard-electric-cooperative.md) for board records, the investigative-audit docket, the rate case, and the Zelenak federal court case. The guide links to the separate source collections so filings, court rulings, and organizational records stay distinct.

## Solar farms and electric connections around Southwest Louisiana

[Browse Louisiana solar permits](state/louisiana-department-of-conservation-and-energy/solar-permits/README.md), [Singer Solar records](indexes/projects/singer-solar.md), and [selected MISO service agreements](other-public-documents/miso/service-agreements/README.md). A permit, public notice, or interconnection agreement has its own scope; read the document before drawing conclusions about a project's status.

## Entergy's Lightning Initiative / the Richland Parish data center

[Open LPSC docket U-37882](state/louisiana-public-service-commission/u-37882/README.md) for public testimony and pre-hearing statements. A separate [Meta Richland Parish page](indexes/projects/meta-richland-data-center.md) links to a company webpage print. The docket filings state parties' positions, not a commission finding.

## Carbon capture, CO2 pipelines, and Pecan Island

[Browse the carbon-capture and pipeline topic page](indexes/topics/carbon-capture-and-pipelines.md). It points to selected state responses, coastal-management records, and pipeline dockets. The Governor's Office production in this archive contains parts 4 and 5 only.

## Waterford 3 water-discharge permit

[Open the Waterford 3 page](indexes/projects/waterford-3.md) for the public notice and draft permit package. The archived package is a draft; this collection does not establish a final permit decision.

## Cajun Crossroads and the Calcasieu Parish Police Jury

[Browse the Louisiana Third Circuit court collection](courts/louisiana/third-circuit/README.md) for the Cajun Crossroads decision. Read the ruling itself for what the court decided; the case record here is selected, not a full docket.

## 1803 Electric and local electric co-ops

[Open the 1803 Electric guide](indexes/organizations/1803-electric-cooperative.md) for commission docket listings, an RFP, planning materials, and selected MISO records. These source types answer different questions and are labeled in their collection pages.
