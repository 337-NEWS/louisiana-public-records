# Archive methodology

## Purpose and scope

This repository preserves source material for public inspection. [337.NEWS](https://337.news) reporting and analysis are separate publications. Inclusion does not verify every claim in a document.

## Provenance and source integrity

Collection READMEs identify the custodian, source, acquisition route, dates, and docket or request numbers where established. A document may be an official agency record, a party's filing, a company publication, or a print of a public webpage. Those classes must remain distinct. "Archived without alteration" means the supplied source file's contents were not edited when added to the archive; neutral archive filenames and Markdown descriptions may be added around it. Where provenance is incomplete, the README says so.

## Publication copies and privacy

Some records are publication-redacted copies. The affected collection README states what was removed and which PDF page was affected; the unredacted copy is not intentionally published here. Reviews consider legally protected material and unnecessary privacy or safety exposure. A redaction is not silently undone by later copies or index text.

## Corrections, replacements, and partial productions

Corrected, supplemental, and replacement records must be identified in collection metadata. Material differences are documented, not silently overwritten. A production labeled with parts, a selected docket, or a request awaiting records is presented as partial or request-only. "Complete" is used only when a source supports that conclusion. Missing records are not reconstructed.

## Reading records in context

An application requests action; a notice announces a process; a draft permit is not a final permit; a company publication states the company's position; a court filing may contain disputed allegations; an order or opinion states the decision of the issuing body within its scope. Neither inclusion nor cross-linking converts allegations into findings. Proposed projects are not described as built without an archived source establishing that fact.

## Navigation and preservation

The archive directories hold canonical records by source. The `indexes/` directory links to them by subject without duplicating files. Source PDFs are not edited for navigation. New metadata and corrections are versioned in Git. For contribution standards, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
