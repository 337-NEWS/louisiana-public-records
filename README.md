# Louisiana Public Records Archive

A public source-document archive maintained by [337.NEWS](https://337.news) for Louisiana government, courts, public infrastructure, regulation, development, and related matters. Reporting and analysis appear on 337.NEWS; this repository preserves the records and neutral context needed to inspect them. Inclusion is not an endorsement of allegations or claims within a document.

## Find records

- [Catalog](CATALOG.md) — every collection, grouped by subject and source.
- [Knowledge indexes](indexes/README.md) — browse by project, person, organization, agency, topic, or infrastructure without duplicating source files.
- [Documented requests](REQUESTS.md) — requests and productions evidenced in this repository.
- [Methodology](METHODOLOGY.md) — provenance, redaction, correction, and preservation practices.
- [Repository structure](REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md) — directory roles and naming rules.

The canonical files remain under `federal/`, `state/`, `parish/`, `municipal/`, and `courts/` by source or custodian. `other-public-documents/` holds identified company, project, and other public publications; collection READMEs distinguish these from agency records.

## Use and contribute

Read documents in context: applications, public notices, draft permits, party filings, rulings, final orders, and company statements serve different purposes. Some archived copies are redacted for publication, and some productions or dockets are partial. Collection READMEs disclose those limits.

Public contributions are moderated under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Preserve provenance, disclose modifications and redactions, and do not silently replace a materially different source record.
