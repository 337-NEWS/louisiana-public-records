## Case summary (from U.S. Attorney's Office releases)

Dusty Guidry, a contract worker in the 15th Judicial District Attorney's Office pretrial-intervention program, pled guilty on March 23, 2023 to two counts of conspiracy to commit bribery concerning programs receiving federal funds and one count of bribery concerning programs receiving federal funds. In October 2025 he was sentenced to 48 months in federal prison plus a $10,000 fine.
