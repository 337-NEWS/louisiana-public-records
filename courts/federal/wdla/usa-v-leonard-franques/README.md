# USA v. Leonard Franques

Court: U.S. District Court, Western District of Louisiana

Case: 6:23-cr-00294 (the U.S. Attorney's Office September 2025 release cites 6:24-cr-00012 for the plea; both numbers are recorded here and have not been reconciled)

Record type: Federal court records (docket reference only; no filings obtained)

## Case summary (from U.S. Attorney's Office releases)

Leonard Franques, a Lafayette business owner, pled guilty on January 12, 2024 to one count of conspiracy to commit bribery concerning programs receiving federal funds. His companies provided the pretrial-intervention program courses that defendants were directed to take, and kickbacks flowed back through the scheme. In October 2025 he was sentenced to 12 months and one day in federal prison plus a $100,000 fine.
