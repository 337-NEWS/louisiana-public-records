# Vernon Parish: Sunstripe Solar and the ordinance

This guide brings together the Vernon Parish Police Jury's posted agendas and draft solar-ordinance amendment, alongside related contextual documents. **KPLC reported the October 23, 2026 vote; official minutes and the executed ordinance were not archived at the collection's last review.** Do not treat an agenda as an adopted measure.
