# Methodology & source integrity

This website is a public navigation layer for the source-document repository maintained by [337.NEWS](https://337.news). Editorial reporting is published separately. The source records remain in their original archival directories and can be inspected at any time.

## What a collection means

Documents are filed by their source or custodian when known. Public-agency records, court filings, corporate publications, third-party article prints, and public webpage captures are identified **as distinct classes of evidence**. An archived company publication is not an agency finding.

## What a document establishes

An application proposes or requests action; an agenda notices items to be considered; a draft is not a final adopted instrument; a court filing may include disputed allegations; an order speaks for the issuing body only within its stated scope. Inclusion in this archive neither authenticates every assertion nor endorses any position.

## Redactions and personal information

Some files are copies redacted for publication. Collection notes describe known modifications where applicable. Do not assume that all personal information appearing in a lawfully obtained public record is material to a published story.

## Partial productions and later corrections

A collection described as **selected**, **partial**, or **request-only** should not be mistaken for a complete governmental response or case docket. Supplemental productions and material replacements should be labeled without silently overwriting earlier records.

## Independent verification

Every downloadable item includes a secondary **Source details** link to its canonical repository path. Consult the adjacent collection README and any available provenance notes or checksums before citing it.
