# DOE Order No. 202-26-50 — Emergency order to PJM Interconnection

U.S. Department of Energy emergency order under Federal Power Act Section 202(c),
directed at PJM Interconnection (the regional transmission organization for 13 states
plus the District of Columbia).

- Issued: October 12, 2026 (announced by Energy Secretary Chris Wright)
- Effective: October 13, 2026 through October 16, 2026
- Purpose as stated by DOE: mitigate blackout risk in the mid-Atlantic during a period
  of elevated demand, high planned generator outages in the autumn maintenance season,
  and numerous planned transmission outages.

The order allows PJM to call on generators above their operating permits as needed
and to direct data centers and other Large Loads onto backup generation when required
for reliability.
