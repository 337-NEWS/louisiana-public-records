## The issuance

- **Docket:** ER26-3515-000 (PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.)
- **FERC citation:** 197 FERC ¶ 61,019
- **Title:** "Order Accepting Tariff Revisions in Part and Rejecting Tariff Revisions in Part"
- **Issued:** October 9, 2026 (notational order, issued Friday evening)
- **Vote:** By the Commission — Chairman Laura V. Swett; Commissioners David Rosner,
  Lindsay S. See, Judy W. Chang, and David LaCerte. No dissent.
  Commissioner Rosner concurred with a separate statement attached to the order.
- **Substance:** Accepted PJM's proposed Large Load definition (50 MW+ cumulative peak
  at a single electrical site, affiliated facilities within one mile counted as one site)
  and the Large Load Registry, effective October 12, 2026. Rejected the proposed
  Interim Resource Adequacy Service (IRAS), the new-capacity designation requirement,
  the Bring Your Own New Capacity (BYONC) rules, and the exclusion of Eligible New
  Large Load from the capacity demand (VRR) curve. Compliance filing due within
  30 days of the order.
