# FERC order in ER26-3515-000 — PJM Interim Resource Adequacy Service (IRAS) proposal

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission order issued October 9, 2026: 197 FERC
paragraph 61,019, Docket No. ER26-3515-000. eLibrary accession 20261009-3055.

The order:
- ACCEPTS PJM's Large Load definition (50+ MW at a single site) and Large Load
  Registry, effective October 12, 2026.
- REJECTS the Interim Resource Adequacy Service (IRAS), the BYONC rules, and the
  related tariff revisions. FERC found key provisions appeared to intrude on state
  authority over retail electricity service.

Commission: Chairman Laura V. Swett; Commissioners Rosner, See, Chang, LaCerte.
No dissent. Commissioner Rosner filed a 2-page concurrence.
