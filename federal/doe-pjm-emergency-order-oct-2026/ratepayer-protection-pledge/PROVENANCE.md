## Key facts established from the documents (for FOIA/request drafting)

- **DOE filing**: Notice of Intervention and Statement of Position, filed **October 7, 2026**, FERC Docket Nos. **ER26-3380-000** (PJM's July 31, 2026 Reliability Backstop Procurement proposal) and **EL26-108-000**. Filed under 42 U.S.C. 7175 (DOE Organization Act § 405) and FERC Rule 214. It is the DOE statement referenced in news coverage; the energy.gov copy is the identical document.
- **FERC order responded to**: September 29, 2026 order, **196 FERC ¶ 61,245 (2026)**, directing PJM to submit revised tariff provisions (FPA § 205) by **October 29, 2026**.
- **Pledge authority cited in the DOE filing**: Proclamation No. 11014, 91 Fed. Reg. 11439 (Mar. 9, 2026) — the Federal Register proclamation declaring the Ratepayer Protection Pledge national policy. Publicly available via federalregister.gov; no FOIA needed.
- **PJM IRAS context**: PJM's IRAS/BYONC filing is **ER26-3515-000** (filed Aug. 13, 2026); per FERC action of Oct. 9, 2026 the IRAS proposal was rejected/deficient, leaving the backstop-procurement docket as the live vehicle. Not downloaded in this pull.
