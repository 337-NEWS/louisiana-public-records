# Ratepayer Protection Pledge materials

The Ratepayer Protection Pledge: voluntary commitments by new large electricity loads
(data centers and similar) to build, bring, or buy their own generation and pay the
full cost, introduced March 2026 and expanded July 2026. Cited authority: Proclamation
No. 11014, 91 Fed. Reg. 11439 (March 9, 2026).

## Files

- `whitehouse-ratepayer-protection-pledge-20260304.html` (plus `.txt` text extraction)
  — official pledge text, published by the White House March 4, 2026
- `FINAL-Ratepayer-Protection-Pledge.pdf` and `.txt` — pledge text as hosted by a
  third-party journalist site (not the official source); PDF and text extraction
- `whitehouse-pledge-signatory-list.html`, `.txt`, and `whitehouse-pledge-signatories-20261015.tsv`
  — 324-entry signatory roster captured from the official White House roster page,
  October 15, 2026. All seven PJM-utility signatories confirmed: American Electric
  Power, AES Corp, CenterPoint Energy (listed as "CenterPointe Energy"), Dominion
  Energy, Exelon Corporation, FirstEnergy, PPL Corporation
- `DOE-notice-of-intervention-statement-of-position-FERC-ER26-3380-EL26-108.pdf` —
  DOE's 9-page filing of October 7, 2026, urging cost-allocation reforms so large-load
  costs are not shifted to ratepayers. IMPORTANT: filed in ER26-3380-000 / EL26-108-000
  (PJM's Reliability Backstop Procurement proposal, filed July 31, 2026), NOT in
  ER26-3515-000
- `doe-statement-webpage.html` (plus `.txt` text extraction) — DOE announcement page,
  published October 8, 2026
- `epa-ratepayer-pledge-expansion-20260723.html` (plus `.txt` text extraction) — EPA
  release on the July 23, 2026 expansion event
- `PROVENANCE.md` — full provenance and gaps
