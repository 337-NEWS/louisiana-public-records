# FOIA request — Phillips 66 Lake Charles emergency SPR exchange

**Filed:** October 23, 2026  
**Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy  
**Recipient:** foia-central@hq.doe.gov  
**Requester:** Dalton Barron / 337.NEWS  
**Status:** Request sent; response pending.

## Request

Records concerning the emergency Strategic Petroleum Reserve exchange authorized by DOE on October 23, 2026 for Phillips 66's Lake Charles refinery.

Requested categories:

1. Phillips 66's written request or other submission seeking the exchange.
2. DOE's approval, authorization, decision memorandum, or equivalent record.
3. The executed exchange agreement, including quantity/grade, delivery, return schedule, and premium-barrel or other repayment terms.
4. Records sufficient to identify the cause of the temporary crude-oil delivery disruption described by DOE.
5. Records sufficient to identify the expected duration and operational consequences represented by Phillips 66.
6. DOE-Phillips 66 communications from October 15 through October 23, 2026 concerning the request, disruption, West Hackberry delivery logistics, urgency, or expected refinery impacts.

The request seeks electronic production, news-media fee classification, reasonably segregable non-exempt material, and advance notice if fees are expected to exceed $25.
