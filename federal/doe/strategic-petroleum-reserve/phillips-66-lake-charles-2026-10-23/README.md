# DOE Strategic Petroleum Reserve — Phillips 66 Lake Charles exchange, October 23, 2026

Official U.S. Department of Energy material concerning the emergency Strategic Petroleum Reserve exchange authorized for Phillips 66's Lake Charles refinery.

## Official sources

## What DOE announced

DOE authorized an emergency exchange of up to 400,000 barrels of crude oil from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to address a crude-oil supply disruption affecting Phillips 66's Lake Charles refinery. DOE identified West Hackberry as the SPR site for immediate delivery and stated that borrowed crude must be returned with additional premium barrels.

DOE's public announcement did not identify the cause of the temporary crude-delivery disruption.

## West Hackberry context

DOE's SPR Quick Facts page reported 23.7 million barrels stored at West Hackberry as of October 16, 2026.
