# FERC CP24-494 — Black Bayou Gas Storage

Public FERC records for Black Bayou Gas Storage, LLC's proposed salt-dome natural-gas storage project in Cameron Parish, Louisiana.

## Source and scope

- Source: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission eLibrary and FERC public notices.
- Docket: CP24-494-000.
- This collection currently indexes the public docket and key agency actions relevant to 337.NEWS reporting. The source files remain available through FERC eLibrary; this collection does not claim to be a complete copy of the docket.

## Key public records

- [FERC eLibrary docket sheet — CP24-494-000](https://elibrary.ferc.gov/eLibrary/docketsheet?chklegadata=false&date_range=custom&date_type=filed_date&docket_number=CP24-494-000&dt_from=1960-01-01&dt_to=2025-07-21&pageNm=dsearch&search_type=docket&sub_docket=All&sub_docket_q=Allsub) — Black Bayou Gas Storage certificate proceeding.
- July 17, 2024 — Black Bayou filed its certificate application. eLibrary docket entries include accession numbers 20240717-5129, 20240717-5130, and 20240717-5131.
- April 18, 2025 — FERC issued the Environmental Assessment for the project (accession 20250418-3010).
- [July 24, 2025 — FERC Notice of Action Taken](https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/decisions-notices/07242025-07242025) — Commission action on CP24-494.
- [July 2025 Commission summary](https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/summaries-july-2025-commission-meeting) — FERC states that the order authorizes Black Bayou Gas Storage, LLC to construct and operate a new salt-dome natural-gas storage project on the Black Bayou salt dome in Cameron Parish.
- [October 20, 2026 delegated-order search](https://elibrary.ferc.gov/eLibrary/search?q=searchText%3D*%26dateType%3Dfiled_date%26startDate%3D2026-10-20%26endDate%3D2026-10-20%26documentClass%3DOrder/Opinion%26documentType%3DDelegated+Order) — accession 20261020-3049, file listed by FERC as `CP24-494 Lim NTP-Mod Approval.pdf`. The order grants Black Bayou's October 5, 2026 request, as supplemented October 9, to commence construction of certain facilities.
