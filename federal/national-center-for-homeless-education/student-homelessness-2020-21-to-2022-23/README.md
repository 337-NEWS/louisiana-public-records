# National Center for Homeless Education — Student Homelessness in America

## Source and scope

- Issuer: National Center for Homeless Education, under a U.S. Department of Education contract.
- Record: *Student Homelessness in America: School Years 2020-21 to 2022-23*. The PDF cover says 2024; the [NCHE publication page](https://nche.ed.gov/student-homelessness-in-america-school-years-2020-21-to-2022-23/) says published 2025.
- Acquired October 27, 2026 from the [NCHE source PDF](https://nche.ed.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NCHE_Student_Homelessness_in_America_SY_2020-21_2022-23.pdf).
- [Download the archived PDF](student-homelessness-in-america.pdf).
- SHA-256: `D6D8D10E15EA6BF399034C0DC8B471304B1685CAD0662E250B7AF9E503A3A0AD`.

The file is an unaltered report based on state-reported federal education data. It covers hotel or motel residence at the point students were identified as experiencing homelessness and includes Louisiana state-level tables. It is not an extended-stay hotel census and does not count every child in hotels.

## Archive status

One selected report; later data may exist.
