# U.S. Attorney's Office, Western District of Louisiana

## Scope

The federal prosecutor's office for the Western District of Louisiana. The archive holds its press releases on the "Operation Cajun Hustle" public-corruption prosecution arising from the 15th Judicial District Attorney's Office. A press release states the office's account; it is not a court filing.
