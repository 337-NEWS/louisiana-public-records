# Substations

The [1803 Electric Cooperative collection](../../other-public-documents/miso/1803-electric-cooperative/README.md) includes a transferred-facilities spreadsheet and expedited review requests for substation and breaker projects. The review requests describe proposals; their presence does not establish approval, completion, or operation. Consult the workbook fields for facility status.
