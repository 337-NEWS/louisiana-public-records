# 1803 Electric Cooperative

The LPSC docket collections for [U-35927](../../state/louisiana-public-service-commission/u-35927/README.md), [U-37134](../../state/louisiana-public-service-commission/u-37134/README.md), and [U-37441](../../state/louisiana-public-service-commission/u-37441/README.md) contain document-list prints from the commission's portal. They identify filings but do not include the listed filings themselves.

The [2025 RFP filing in LPSC X-37665](../../state/louisiana-public-service-commission/x-37665/README.md) is an official docket record. [Third-party articles and a nonprofit-data print](../../other-public-documents/1803-electric-third-party-publications/README.md) are separately labeled publisher materials, not agency findings.

The [2026 LPSC IRP initiation filing](../../state/louisiana-public-service-commission/1803-electric-2026-irp-initiation/README.md) is a separate collection of the cooperative's request and proposed exhibits. It cites the [corrected IRP general order](../../state/louisiana-public-service-commission/r-36262/README.md).

The [MISO collection](../../other-public-documents/miso/1803-electric-cooperative/README.md) contains selected agreements, public planning criteria, facility data, project review requests, and a meeting notice involving 1803 Electric Cooperative. Project review requests are proposals, not final approvals.
