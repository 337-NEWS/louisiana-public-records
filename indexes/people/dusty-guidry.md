# Dusty Guidry

## Scope

Contract worker in the 15th Judicial District Attorney's Office pretrial-intervention program. Pled guilty March 23, 2023 to conspiracy and bribery charges concerning programs receiving federal funds; sentenced October 2025 to 48 months in federal prison plus a $10,000 fine.
