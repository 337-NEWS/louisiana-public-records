# Gary Haynes

## Scope

Former Assistant District Attorney in the 15th Judicial District Attorney's Office (Lafayette, Acadia, and Vermilion parishes). Convicted by a federal jury in September 2025 on six counts arising from a bribery and kickback scheme tied to the office's pretrial-intervention program; sentenced December 12, 2025 to seven years in federal prison plus a $200,000 fine.
