# Leonard Franques

## Scope

Lafayette business owner whose companies provided pretrial-intervention program courses in the 15th Judicial District. Pled guilty January 12, 2024 to conspiracy to commit bribery concerning programs receiving federal funds; sentenced October 2025 to 12 months and one day in federal prison plus a $100,000 fine.
