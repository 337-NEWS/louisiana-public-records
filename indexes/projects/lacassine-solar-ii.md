# Lacassine Solar II

## Overview

Two MISO PDFs identify Lacassine Solar II, LLC, Entergy Louisiana, LLC, and MISO as parties to service agreement 4059, queue project J1795. The files are distinct revisions of the same agreement, retained separately.

## Timeline

- October 21, 2024: [First revised generator interconnection agreement](../../other-public-documents/miso/service-agreements/entergy-la_lacassine-solar-ii_j1795-sa4059_1strev.pdf), effective date printed on its cover.
- May 29, 2025: [Second revised generator interconnection agreement](../../other-public-documents/miso/service-agreements/entergy-la_lacassine-solar_j1795-sa4059_2ndrev.pdf), effective date printed on its cover.

## Known entities

The named parties are Lacassine Solar II, LLC, Entergy Louisiana, LLC, and MISO. The project name and queue number differ from [Lacassine Solar, J1669](lacassine-solar.md).

## Open documentary questions

The agreements do not, by themselves, establish construction, energization, or operation. No separate agency permit is linked here.
