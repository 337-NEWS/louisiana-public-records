# Mowata Solar

## Overview

The archive contains a MISO generator interconnection agreement naming Mowata Solar, LLC, and a Louisiana immediate solar permit labeled Mowata Solar. These are different record types: contractual interconnection terms and an agency permit.

## Timeline

- April 24, 2023: [MISO generator interconnection agreement, SA 4047 / J1794](../../other-public-documents/miso/service-agreements/entergy-la_mowata-solar_gia-sa4047.pdf), effective date on the cover.
- November 7, 2025: [Louisiana Immediate Solar Permit ISP00009](../../state/louisiana-department-of-conservation-and-energy/solar-permits/2025-11-07-isp00009-mowata-solar.pdf).

## Known entities

The MISO agreement names Mowata Solar, LLC, Entergy Louisiana, LLC, and MISO. The state permit identifies its own recipient and conditions.
