# Pecan Island Area Project

## Overview
The Office of Coastal Management collection documents an ExxonMobil Low Carbon Solutions Onshore Storage LLC proposal and a coastal-use review record. A separate USACE FOIA request asks for records involving the former ExxonMobil Pecan Island property.

## Known entities
The coastal-management collection identifies ExxonMobil Low Carbon Solutions Onshore Storage LLC as applicant.

## Geography
Vermilion Parish, according to the state collection.
