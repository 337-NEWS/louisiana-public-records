# Starbase Louisiana / Pecan Island records

## Overview
Two archived FOIA requests seek federal cultural-resource and Section 106 records associated with Starbase Louisiana and Pecan Island. A separate, externally hosted 38-page CPRA records production documents Louisiana Economic Development's Project Osprey planning and interagency correspondence from March–August 2026. The FOIA requests document the scope of those requests, not agency findings or a completed project.

## Known entities
The requests name the FAA and USACE as requested agencies and refer to SpaceX/Starbase Louisiana.

## Geography
Pecan Island and Vermilion Parish are named in the requests.
