# Sulphur City Council — selected 2026 records

- Jurisdiction: Municipal — City of Sulphur, Louisiana
- Source body: Sulphur City Council
- Record types: Agendas, meeting minutes, agenda and resolutions/ordinances packets, an ordinance print, and mayoral budget message
- Archive status: Selected records; not a complete 2026 meeting series
- Provenance: PDFs supplied by Dalton Barron in `sulphur-city-hall-records-20261012.zip` and its expanded `(1)` ZIP. The accompanying manifests describe retrieval from City of Sulphur document pages or archived copies. Those retrieval claims have not been independently verified for this collection. The source PDFs are preserved byte-for-byte.

## Gaps

The supplied ZIPs do not include every 2026 agenda or set of minutes. The manifests are source-sweep notes and are not treated as City of Sulphur records. Three campaign/ethics PDFs in the expanded ZIP were not added because they include residential address information.
