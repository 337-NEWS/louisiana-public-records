# SWLA Corporate Contacts

Compiled October 14, 2026. Research notes, not public records: each file lists companies operating in Beauregard Parish and Southwest Louisiana, their decision makers, and contact information published on company sites, in press releases, or in public filings. No email address was invented or guessed.

**Note:** these files contain business contact emails. They are published business addresses, included here as compiled research.

## Files

- [swla-executive-contacts.md](swla-executive-contacts.md) — 19 companies: timber, LNG/energy, utilities, and industrial landholders. 9 yielded public emails.
- [swla-executive-contacts-expanded.md](swla-executive-contacts-expanded.md) — 23 more companies: smaller timber operators, pipeline land/ROW contacts, retail site selection, rail and industrial.
- [swla-executive-contacts-chains.md](swla-executive-contacts-chains.md) — 44 chain businesses: QSR, coffee, grocery, convenience/fuel, auto, banks, storage, car wash, hotels, with site-selection contacts and published site criteria.
- [swla-executive-contacts-creative.md](swla-executive-contacts-creative.md) — ~80 companies and entities: drive-thru coffee, more QSR, distressed-retail disposition brokers (Hilco, Gordon Brothers, A&G), cell towers, billboards, solar and data-center developers, timberland sellers, aggregates, RV parks, mitigation banking, carbon.

Entries for 337.news investigation subjects are marked EXCLUDED and were removed from outreach consideration. Entries the researcher was told to drop are marked KILLED with the reason and date.
