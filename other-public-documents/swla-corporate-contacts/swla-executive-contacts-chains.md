## Best targets, ranked (land-job fit)

1. **Harbor Freight Tools** — realestate@harborfreight.com (published department inbox on their own RE page) + published criteria (15,000–16,500 SF box, excellent visibility, WB-65 truck access). Stores are company-owned (no franchise layer), territory RE Managers assigned by region, and their rural/small-market footprint matches Beauregard Parish. Cleanest site-submission funnel on the list.
2. **Buc-ee's** — Buys 25–36 acres outright per travel center ($60–95M all-in builds). I-10 corridor strategy fits SWLA; Ruston, LA breaks ground 2027 and Lafayette, LA is in the 2028 pipeline, so Louisiana is active. Named RE team exists (Stan Beard, Director of Real Estate and Development; Richard Golden, VP RE Development; Derrek Stevens, Sr. VP RE) but no published email or portal. Realistic route: local brokers and economic-development groups (that's how their deals get placed). Biggest per-deal land buyer on the board.
3. **RaceTrac** — The only chain with a public submit-a-property portal AND full published criteria (1.5–2+ acres, 5,500 SF prototype, 18–24 fueling stations, high AADT, excellent access/visibility). Casey Andrews, Sr. RE Rep for New Store Growth (name/title only, no published email). Expanding throughout the South. racetrac.com/About-Us/Real-Estate/Submit.
4. **Walk-On's Sports Bistreaux** — Baton Rouge HQ, actively franchising. Kelly Parker, Director of Franchise Sales & Real Estate, 225-330-4533 (published in company press releases); franchise@walk-ons.com. Most directly reachable Louisiana-born chain on the list.
5. **ALDI** — Published criteria (2.5-acre pads, ±22,000 SF building, 103' min frontage, signalized full-access intersection, 20,000+ VPD) + official state-by-state RE contact portal (corporate.aldi.us/real-estate). Proven recent Louisiana land buyer: 3 acres at the former Cortana Mall site, Baton Rouge, March 2024 (via ELIFIN broker Will Chadwick, 225-368-7667 — a proven local intermediary for both ALDI and Starbucks). LA territory is currently blank on ALDI's published contact list; neighboring-territory reps with published @aldi.us emails are flagged below.

Honorable mentions: **Huey Magoo's** (real published emails + new condensed 1,500-SF drive-thru prototype with a smaller land footprint — fits smaller-market parcels); **PJ's Coffee** (LA-born, named internal RE team: Ryan Robin, Real Estate Manager; Christopher Pierson, VP Real Estate & Construction; franchise@pjscoffee.com from the public FDD); **CubeSmart/Brookwood via Mark Hebert, Kurz & Hebert** (the Baton Rouge broker running CubeSmart's Louisiana land-buying; ~$5–6M builds).

## Notes for outreach

- Dalton's email rule: license info goes in the signature, not the body. All cold outreach; keep bodies short.
- The funnel quality ladder on this list: **department inbox + criteria (Harbor Freight, RaceTrac portal, ALDI portal) > published named human (Huey Magoo's, Walk-On's) > franchise-route human (PJ's, CC's) > broker/intermediary (Buc-ee's RE team, CubeSmart via Hebert) > media-only (no)**. Never use a media-relations contact for a real-estate pitch.
- Franchise-heavy brands (most QSR): corporate is not the door. The practical land door is multi-unit developers and their tenant-rep brokers — cultivate those broker relationships (Pinpoint CRE, Gill Properties) rather than cold-emailing franchising inboxes.
- Verification needed before any outreach: Bob Flavi's email (2020 press release); americas.development@ihg.com (2016 FDD); the Fort Collins filing email bradsanders@raisingcanes.com (project-only, not standing RE email); the 2014 AutoZone regional list (discard).
