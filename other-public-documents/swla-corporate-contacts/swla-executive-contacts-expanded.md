## Notes for outreach

- Dalton's email rule: license info goes in the signature, not the body. All cold outreach; keep bodies short.
- The strongest pattern in this batch: **named humans with published emails cluster in retail site selection (O'Reilly)** and **project inboxes cluster in pipelines (Texas Gateway)**. For everyone else, the ask should be routable: "who handles your company's land acquisitions in Southwest Louisiana?"
- Pipeline companies are most valuable to a land agent when actively buying easements: Gulf South's Texas Gateway is the one live event in Beauregard Parish. After it, Williams Line 200 construction activity.
- TIMOs (Molpus, FIA) buy and sell timberland constantly at institutional scale; Mark James's LA broker license makes him the most agent-compatible contact in the batch.
- George Swift (SWLA Alliance) is the only person on either list positioned to SEND Dalton business (inbound industrial tenants), not just receive a pitch.
- Airport tenant roster: not published anywhere; call Sam Lack at (337) 463-8250 for the current list.
