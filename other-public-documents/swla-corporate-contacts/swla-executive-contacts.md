## Notes for outreach

- Dalton's email rule: license info goes in the signature, not the body. Every company here is cold outreach, so keep bodies short.

A "cold email to the CEO" will, in practice, land with a comms person or executive assistant at 8 of 9 companies. The ask should be routable: "who handles your company's land acquisitions in Southwest Louisiana?"
