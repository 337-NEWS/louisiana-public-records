# Public-records request — Black Bayou Gas Storage ITEP records

**Filed:** October 23, 2026  
**Agency:** Cameron Parish Police Jury  
**Recipient:** julie@cameronda.org  
**Requester:** Dalton Barron / 337.NEWS  
**Status:** Request sent; response pending.

## Request

Records concerning Black Bayou Gas Storage Hub, LLC and the Industrial Tax Exemption Program resolution considered at the August 13, 2026 Cameron Parish Police Jury special meeting.

Requested categories:

1. Records provided to or prepared for jurors before the vote, including the ITEP application, advance notification, project summary, investment figures, exemption terms, and supporting material.
2. Records sufficient to show estimated property taxes exempted or affected and taxes expected to remain payable.
3. Records sufficient to show projected permanent jobs, construction jobs, payroll, local hiring commitments, or other employment representations.
4. Economic-impact, fiscal-impact, cost-benefit, staff, spreadsheet, briefing, or presentation material.
5. Written communications from January 1 through August 13, 2026 between parish officials/staff and Black Bayou, its representatives, or Louisiana Economic Development concerning the ITEP request, investment, taxes, jobs, local benefits, or conditions.
6. Records reflecting infrastructure, road, drainage, emergency-response, public-service, community-benefit, mitigation, or other commitments represented to the Parish in connection with the request.

The request excludes privileged legal advice and unrelated records, seeks electronic production, and asks the custodian to cite any exemption used to withhold responsive material.
