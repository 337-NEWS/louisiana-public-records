# Cameron Parish Police Jury — Black Bayou Gas Storage

Official Cameron Parish Police Jury webpages and linked public documents concerning Black Bayou Gas Storage.

## May 2026 FERC amendment notice

Cameron Parish published a public notice concerning Black Bayou's request to amend its federal certificate in FERC Docket CP26-257-000.

- [Parish notice page](https://cameronpj.org/public-notice-and-intervention-deadline-for-black-bayou-gas-storage-caverns-amendment-project/)
- [Black Bayou stakeholder / FERC public notice PDF](https://cameronpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FERQ-Public-Notice.pdf)
- [FERC landowner informational handout PDF](https://cameronpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Informational-Handout.pdf)
- [FERC Office of Public Participation — motion-to-intervene instructions PDF](https://cameronpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Motions-to-Intervene-Steps.pdf)

The parish notice identifies CP26-257-000 as an amendment request to add storage capacity and states that copies of the application were available at the Cameron Parish Public Library.

## August 13, 2026 special meeting — ITEP

- [Official special-meeting page](https://cameronpj.org/august-13-2026-special-meeting/)

The published agenda called for a resolution recommending an Industrial Tax Exemption for Black Bayou Gas Storage Hub, LLC at 1642 Gum Cove Road, Hackberry. The agenda note listed estimated capital investment of $685,044,649.
