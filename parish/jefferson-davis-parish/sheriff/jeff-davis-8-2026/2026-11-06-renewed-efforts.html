Renewed Efforts in the Jeff Davis 8 Investigations

Date Posted: 11/06/2026

The new JDPSO administration, under Sheriff Kyle C. Miers, has always made these cases a top priority and we will continue efforts to bring renewed attention to the Jeff Davis 8 investigation. The digitization of evidence is an important step in preserving investigative materials, making information more accessible, and exploring new opportunities to move the investigation forward.


We remain committed to supporting these efforts and pursuing answers for the victims and their families.
