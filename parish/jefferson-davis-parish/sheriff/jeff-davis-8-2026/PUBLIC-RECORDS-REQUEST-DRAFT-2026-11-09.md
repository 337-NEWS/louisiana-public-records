# Draft public-records request — evidence preservation procurement

Status: draft only; not submitted. Prepared November 9, 2026.

To: Custodian of records, Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office.

Please provide existing, publicly releasable administrative records dated January 1 through November 9, 2026 concerning the preservation or digitization effort described in the Sheriff's Office's November 6, 2026 statement about the Jeff Davis 8 investigations:

1. Contracts, purchase orders, vendor statements of work, invoices, grant applications or awards, and budget appropriations for digitizing, inventorying, or preserving historical recordings or case materials.
2. Publicly releasable policies or project plans describing the preservation workflow, inventory method, and handling of original recordings or physical media.
3. Publicly releasable interagency agreements or authorizations that identify agencies or vendors permitted to handle or digitize those materials and the custodial agency. Administrative terms and signatories suffice; protected investigative details may be redacted.
4. Public press statements or releases on this effort from October 26 through November 9, 2026.

This request excludes evidence content, victim or witness personal information, confidential case files, sealed material, and records whose release would compromise an active investigation. Please identify any responsive administrative record withheld and the legal basis, and provide segregable portions where possible. Electronic copies are preferred; no new analysis is requested.
