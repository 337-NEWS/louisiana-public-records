# Public-records request — Sunstripe Solar / Vernon Parish solar moratorium and ordinance

**Filed:** October 23, 2026  
**Agency:** Vernon Parish Police Jury  
**Recipient:** publicrecords@vppjla.com  
**Requester:** Dalton Barron / 337.NEWS  
**Status:** Request sent; response pending.

## Request

Records concerning the Vernon Parish solar-farm moratorium, the October 23, 2026 solar ordinance amendment, and the proposed Sunstripe / Treaty Oak solar project.

Requested categories:

1. The complete executed copy of Resolution No. 38 of 2026, adopted August 10, 2026, establishing the temporary moratorium on solar farms in unincorporated Vernon Parish.
2. The complete executed and signed copy of the solar-farm ordinance amendment adopted October 23, 2026, including exhibits, attachments, certification, ordinance number, and publication notice.
3. Official minutes, roll-call vote records, or other records sufficient to show the disposition and vote on the ordinance amendment and the motion to rescind Resolution No. 38 at the October 23 special meeting.
4. Official minutes for the August 10 special call meeting, September 21 public hearing/meeting, and October 19 regular meeting, to the extent prepared or approved.
5. Any application, site-development permit application, solar-farm permit application, site plan, development plan, supporting exhibits, fee submission, or other formal filing submitted to Vernon Parish by Treaty Oak Clean Energy, LLC, TO LA Solar 1, LLC, Sunstripe, Galehead Development, or any representative or affiliate for the project.
6. Written materials submitted to the Police Jury by or on behalf of those entities from July 1 through October 23, 2026 concerning the project, including presentations, maps, project descriptions, capacity figures, acreage figures, schedules, decommissioning information, road-use information, or responses to juror questions.
7. Records sufficient to identify whether Vernon Parish has received or accepted any parish solar-farm/site-development permit application for Sunstripe as of the date the request is processed. If none exists, written confirmation that no responsive record exists would satisfy this item.

The request seeks electronic production and asks the custodian to identify any statutory exemption used to withhold responsive material.
