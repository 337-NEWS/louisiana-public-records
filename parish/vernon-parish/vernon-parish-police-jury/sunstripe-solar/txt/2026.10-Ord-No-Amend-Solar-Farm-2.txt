                                       ORDINANCE

              AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND ORDINANCE NO. 9 of 2025

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING REGULATIONS FOR THE SITE DEVELOPMENT
 OF LAND IN THE UNINCORPORATED AREA OF VERNON PARISH FOR THE
  PURPOSE OF CONSTRUCTING AND OPERATING "SOLAR FARMS;" AND,
           FURTHER PROVIDING WITH RESPECT THERETO.

WHEREAS,              it is apparent that the unincorporated portion of Vernon Parish includes
                      areas that are suitable for the development of large "solar farms" using
                      arrays of devices to collect energy from the sun for the purpose of
                      generating electric power;

WHEREAS,              such solar farms present a unique need for Site Development
                      regulations given the acreage involved in such developments, the
                      characteristics of the structures constructed and placed on these
                      properties, the potential impact on neighboring properties, the
                      potential impact on vehicle traffic, and other factors;

WHEREAS,              the present site development requirements applicable in the
                      unincorporated area of Vernon Parish does not fully address the
                      unique aspects of the development of sites for the establishment of
                      solar farms; and,

WHEREAS,              the Vernon Parish Police Jury finds that additional regulations of
                      the development of sites for solar farms is in the interest of the citizens
                      of Vernon Parish;

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED that the Compiled Ordinances of Vernon Parish
              be, and are hereby, supplemented and amended to add "Site
              Development Regulations for Solar Farms," providing as follows:

               Site Development Regulations for Solar Farms
Purpose.
The purpose of this Ordinance is to provide additional Site Development regulations
necessary to address unique circumstances presented by the development of sites for the
construction, establishment and operation of "Solar Farms'

Definitions.
For the purposes of this Ordinance the following terms shall mean:

Solar energy means radiant energy received from the sun that can be collected in the form of
heat or light by a solar collector.

Solar energy system means a device or structural design feature intended to provide for
collection, storage, and distribution of solar energy for heating or cooling, electricity
generation, or water heating.

Solar farm means the use of land where a series of solar collectors and related equipment
and accessories are placed in an area on a parcel of land for the purpose of generating
photovoltaic power which has a generation capacity of at least one (l) megawatt (MW) direct
current (dc) or more when operating at maximum efficiency. Solar farm shall not be
interpreted to mean one or more solar collectors intended to provide electrical power
generation for a single residential dwelling or commercial property. Accessories may include
maintenance buildings, electric transmission lines, substations, energy storage equipment,
and related accessory uses and structures.

Opaque visual barrier means a barrier that is created by dense tree cover with an
uninterrupted line of trees and shrubs, earthen berms or solid fencing or combination of any
of these elements designed to comply block the view between two (2) areas.

Permits and fees.

           a) Before commencing the construction, placement, establishment, expansion, or
              substantive alteration of a solar farm the owner of an existing solar farm or
              the developer of a new solar farm shall require a Site Development Permit
              from the Vernon Parish Police Jury in accordance with the requirements of
              this Ordinance.

           b) Applications for site development permits for solar farms shall include a site
              plan showing all property to be included in the project, all access roads,
              drainage study, environmental study, and a traffic plan. The applicant shall
              conduct at least one community meeting to provide adjacent landowners and
              the public an opportunity to ask questions and discuss the project. The
              community meeting shall be conducted prior to permit approval. Notice of the
              public meeting shall be published at least twice in the official journal, and the
              meeting shall be held at a location in the parish suitable for public meetings.

           c) The applicant shall include with the permit application the plan for the solar
              farm that meets the requirements provided for in this Ordinance.

           d) Upon submission of a permit application for a Solar Farm the applicant shall
              pay a fee to the parish in the sum of one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) plus
              five dollars ($5.00) per acre. The applicable permit fee shall be determined by
              consideration of the entire project, inclusive of buffer zones and access roads.

           e) An annual monitoring and maintenance fee beginning the year after issuance
              of the permit and every year thereafter not to exceed two thousand five
              hundred dollars ($2,500.00).

           f) If, at any time, the parish requires the assistance of an expert(s) to assist in
              reviewing and verifying siting requirements, the applicant will be responsible
              to pay all reasonable expert’s fees.

           g) Review and issuance of the application for a permit hereunder shall be by the
              Parish Administrator and the Parish Road Manager, with the concurrence of
              the Vernon Parish Police Jury. If the application is denied at any step of the
              review process, the official issuing such denial shall provide a written
              statement as to the reasons for the denial to the applicant and the applicant
              shall be afforded thirty (30) days to submit additional information or an
              amended plan to remediate the defects that were the basis for the denial. If the
              application is finally denied, the applicant shall have the option to appeal the

                denial to the Police Jury, and the Police Jury may, by majority vote, direct the
                Administrator to grant the permit.

           h) A permit issued under this Ordinance may be transferred or assigned, with the
              assignee being obligated to all requirements of the permit and this chapter;
              however, written notice of such transfer or assignment shall be made to the
              parish and the identity of the new permittee shall be noted in the parish
              records.

           i) The issuance of a permit under this Ordinance shall serve as the agreement
              and acknowledgment by the permittee, and its successors and assigns, as well
              as the property owners, that the Vernon Parish Police Jury shall have stood to
              enforce any and all provisions and obligations of the permit.

           j) A permit shall expire twenty-four (24) months from the date of issuance,
              unless either work has commenced on the solar farm that is of a nature and
              character that reflects a good faith intention to continue the work until
              completion or, upon application of the developer, the Parish Administrator
              grants an extension. After the expiration of a permit for failure to commence
              work within said twenty-four (24) months, the applicant may reapply.

Single or multiple tracts.

           a) A solar farm may be operated on a single contiguous tract or multiple non-
              contiguous tracts, either with ownership by applicant, under one or more
              leases in which applicant is lessee, or any combination thereof.

           b) Any permit issued for a solar farm which relies upon one or more lease
              agreements shall become null and void upon the termination of any such lease
              agreement with respect to the land subject to the terminated lease. This
              provision shall not apply when the lease agreement is terminated upon the
              permittee/lessee taking ownership of the property previously subject to the
              lease. This provision shall not apply when the permittee/lessee can
              demonstrate to the Parish engineer’s reasonable satisfaction that the Solar
              Farm plans and specifications can be modified to accommodate the permitted
              Solar Farm without the use of the property that is the subject of the terminated
              lease.

Traffic plan.

           a) The plan for a proposed solar farm shall include a traffic plan.

           b) The traffic plan must be approved by the Parish Road Manager prior to the
              issuance of the permit.

           c) Whenever possible, contractors should construct and use a temporary access
              road during the construction of the project to mitigate the impact on nearby
              residences and damage to the local roads.

           d) The plan shall account for the movement of vehicles that will use public roads
              during the construction, maintenance and decommissioning of the solar farm.
              Temprary Traffic Control signs are required under any plan to be placed in
              advance of the points of ingress and egress from temporary access roads /
              entries / exits onto a Parish Road and from a Parish Road on to a State
              Highway within one (1) mile of the permitted site. Temporary Traffic Control
              signs warning of construction area and slow moving traffic ahead are required
              to be thirty-six (36) inch by thirty-six (36) inch signs along Parish Roads and
              posted five hundred (500) feet in advance of the point of ingress and egress.
              Temporary Traffic Control signs are required along State Highways and are
              required to be forty-eight (48) inch by forty-eight (48) inch and placed one-
              thousand (1,000) feet and five-hundred (500) feet in advance of the point of
              ingress or egress, which includes from a permitted Parish Road use to a State

             Highway. All Temporary Traffic Control signs are required to be orange in

          e) The plan shall provide for the remediation of any damages caused by the solar
             farm project occasioned to public roads during the construction, maintenance
             and decommissioning of the solar farm. Applicant is 100% responsible for the
             cost and repair of any damages occasioned to public roads attributed to the
             project during the construction, maintenance and decommissioning of the
             solar farm

Drainage plan.

          a) The plan for a proposed solar farm shall include a drainage plan.

          b) The drainage plan shall include hydrologic and hydraulic (H&H) analysis to
             establish that the solar farm will not have any adverse impact on the parish
             drainage system or adjacent property owners; alternatively, the drainage plan
             shall establish a plan for remediating any adverse impact on the parish
             drainage system or adjacent property owners that may result from the
             construction of and the operation of the solar farm.

Buffer zones and vicinage.

          a) The plan for a proposed solar farm shall include the provision of a buffer
              zone around the perimeter of the solar farm.

          b) The buffer zone shall consist of a setback of at least one hundred fifty (150)
              300 feet from any public road as measured from the edge of the currently
              maintained road right of way. fifty (50) feet from the front line of any
              adjacent property (as configured at the time of the application); and, twenty-
              five (25) feet from the rear or side line of an adjacent property.

             and
              The buffer zone shall consist of a setback of at least five hundred (500) feet
              from the property line of any private property (this includes, but is not limited
              to, residences, privately owned agricultural land, or any other occupied
              structure.

              In the event that a five hundred (500) foot buffer zone impacts an already
              existing business, the facility owner MUST consult with the property owner
              and come to an agreement that will satisfy the property owner and facility
              operator.

              and

              Private property owners may, at their discretion, enter into a separate written
              agreement with the facility operator to allow for a lesser setback adjacent to
              their property. This agreement shall be submitted to the Vernon Parish Police
              Jury as part of the permit application process.

          c) The buffer zone shall be maintained in such a manner so as to not present a
              nuisance as provided for otherwise in the Code of Ordinances. Maintenance
              thereof shall be subject to the enforcement provisions of the Code of
              Ordinances in regard to nuisances and abatement thereof.

          d) No structures or equipment shall be placed in the buffer zone except for
              fences or walls constructed of new materials and underground electrical or
              communication lines. Trees or other vegetation may be placed in the buffer
              zone. Access roads and overhead electrical or communication lines may be
              placed in a buffer zone upon approval of the Vernon Parish Police Jury.

          e) Structures or equipment outside the buffer zones shall be designed,
              constructed, or situated so as to prevent any glare, noise (no more than 10
              dBA above ambient noise levels as measured from the property line), heat,
              odors or the like emitted from or by such structure or equipment having an
              unreasonable impact on neighboring properties, including public roads.

          f) Screening at least six feet (6') in height consisting of vegetation, earthen
              berms, or solid fencing (or a combination of these elements) is required in the
              buffer zone adjacent to the curtilage of any existing residence property line of
              adjacent property owners. If a vegetative barrier is the sole barrier, it shall be
              comprised of native vegetation, shall ensure an opaque barrier year-round,
              and shall be required for permitting. Existing vegetation on site may be
              retained undisturbed in satisfaction of the vegetative barrier requirement if it
              otherwise meets requirements of this section. A sole vegetative barrier shall
              be a thirty-five (35) foot deep vegetative composed of new plant material or a
              fifty (50) foot deep vegetative barrier composed of natural plant material.

          g) An opaque barrier is required in the buffer zone adjacent to the property line
              of adjacent property owners.

Secured access and lighting.

          a) The plan for a proposed solar farm shall include a plan for sufficient access
             for first responders during both construction and operation.

          b) The plan for a proposed solar farm shall include the minimum lighting
             necessary for the safe operation of the solar farm and shall be designed to
             limit, to the extent practicable, the impact of artificial lighting on adjacent
             residences, buildings, roadways or properties.

          c) Perimeter fencing must comply with National Electric Safety Code or other
             applicable safety code standards. Where practicable, applicants should
             strongly consider fencing options which are aesthetically fitting to
             surrounding area (i.e. "high fence" in rural locations).

Decommissioning plan.

          a) The plan for a proposed solar farm shall include a decommissioning plan for
             the restoration of the area of the solar farm upon the cessation of operations of
             the solar farm.

          b) Cessation of operations and maintenance, without prior written notice to the
             parish, for a period exceeding one hundred and eighty (180) consecutive days
             shall trigger the decommissioning plan and termination of the permit. If
             permittee provides written notice to the parish, within the 180-day period
             providing an anticipated timeline for resumption of operations, the
             decommissioning requirement shall not be triggered, and the parish’s
             approval of such extension shall not be unreasonably withheld.

          c) The decommissioning plan shall provide for the removal of the solar panels,
             ancillary structures, and other infrastructure utilized in the operation of the
             solar farm and shall specify the timeline for such actions. Failure to adhere to
             the plan, including meeting the included timeline, may subject the permittee
             to liquidated damages as follows: In the event an inspection notes a failure to
             adhere to the plan, the parish shall provide written notice to the permittee of
             the deficiency, which notice shall be deemed provided upon delivery by mail
             or electronic means. The permittee shall have sixty (60) days from the date of
             delivery of the notice to remediate the deficiency. If the permittee fails to
             remediate the deficiency within said sixty-day (60-day) period, the permittee
             agrees to pay a penalty to the parish of five hundred dollars ($500.00) for
             each subsequent day, or part thereof, that the deficiency is not remediated,
             with each day considered a separate violation.

           d) The decommissioning plan shall provide for remediation of any
              environmental hazards resulting from natural disasters.

           e) The decommissioning plan shall provide for the remediation of any
              environmental hazards caused by the former solar farm, as determined by the
              EPA, DEQ, or the Vernon Parish Police Jury.

           f) A showing that any lease forming a portion of the application for the original
              permit includes (or incorporates by reference) a sufficient decommissioning
              plan and the funding thereof as a necessary term or condition.

          g) A decommission bond in an amount sufficient to ensure proper site closure.
             Any bond shall be executed by the permittee and a corporate surety licensed
             to do business in the state. The bond or other instrument shall be payable to
             the Vernon Parish Police Jury. The decommissioning bond shall be re-
             evaluated every five (5) three (3) years.

Maintenance plan.

       a) A maintenance plan shall be submitted as part of the permit application process.
          The plan shall include a schedule for the regular maintenance in place of the solar
          farm and buffer zone including but not limited to grass cutting, shrubbery and
          tree maintenance, chemical or herbicides/pesticides application (and justification
          for use), and general upkeep of the premises. The maintenance plan shall include
          provisions providing for the removal of any trees or shrubs planted as a
          vegetative screen that die or become significantly degraded and the replacement
          of such trees or shrubs with trees/shrubs of a like or comparable kind. All
          landscaping must be maintained and warrantied for the life of the facility. Any
          plant material deemed unhealthy must be replaced immediately. Any chemical
          that is not approved for agricultural application shall not be sprayed for
          maintenance of a solar generation facility site.

       b) Adherence to the plan is subject to periodic and annual inspections by the parish,
          and the parish may require adherence to the plan in regard to maintenance.

       c) In the event an inspection notes a failure to adhere to the plan, the parish shall
          provide written notice to the permittee of the deficiency, which notice shall be
          deemed provided upon delivery by mail or electronic means. The permittee shall
          have thirty (30) days from the date of delivery of the notice to remediate the
          deficiency. If the permittee fails to remediate the deficiency within said thirty-day
          (30-day) period, the permittee agrees to pay a penalty to the parish of five
          hundred dollars ($500.00) for each subsequent day, or part thereof, that the
          deficiency is not remediated, with each day considered a separate violation.

       d) The parish may also enforce the plan through the existing parish ordinances
          governing the abatement of nuisances.

Regulations.

       Facility owner must comply with all Federal and State guidelines related to
       environmental regulations, water runoff, water drainage, etc.

       If, at any time during either construction or operation of the solar farm, there is an
       adverse effect on any adjoining property owner, facility owner MUST work with the
       property owner to mitigate and correct the issue.
