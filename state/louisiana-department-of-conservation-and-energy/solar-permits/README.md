# Louisiana solar permits

- Issuing body: Louisiana Department of Energy and Natural Resources for the September 2025 permits; Louisiana Department of Conservation and Energy for the later permits, as named on the documents.
- Record types: Immediate Solar Permits (`ISP`) and Solar Siting Permits (`SSP`).
- Provenance: PDF copies supplied by Dalton Barron. The permit documents identify the issuing department, permit number, and recipient. Files are archived byte-for-byte without alteration.
