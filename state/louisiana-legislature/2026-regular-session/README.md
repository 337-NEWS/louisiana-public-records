# Louisiana Legislature — 2026 regular session

- Source: Louisiana Legislature
- Provenance: PDFs supplied to this archive by Dalton Barron. Version labels reflect the supplied documents.

## Files

- `hcr-59-original.pdf` — Original version of House Concurrent Resolution 59 concerning the state climate action plan.
- `hcr-61-original.pdf` — Original version of House Concurrent Resolution 61 concerning a study of property rights, expropriation, and carbon sequestration.
- `hr-144-enrolled.pdf` — Enrolled House Resolution 144 requesting a Board of Regents study concerning carbon stream mixing.
- `hr-286-original.pdf` — Original version of House Resolution 286 concerning a rural community energy and education study.

“Original” identifies an introduced version, not the final text or final disposition. HCR 61 and HR 286 were subsequently amended. Check the [Louisiana Legislature](https://legis.la.gov/) for later versions and legislative status.
